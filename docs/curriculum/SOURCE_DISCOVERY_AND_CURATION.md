# Source Discovery and Curation

## Purpose

AtoEnglish must find and curate authentic media before the learner sees it. The learner chooses among a small reviewed set; the learner does not search raw YouTube.

This document governs the first A0 corpus task. It does not authorize publication of any candidate.

## Corpus target

The first seven-day validation slice targets:

- 20–30 reviewed Communication Clips;
- five A0 capabilities;
- 3–5 clips per capability;
- multiple speakers and contexts;
- at least one anchor, interaction, and cold-transfer role per capability;
- delayed recall and unseen-speaker evidence.

These counts remain pilot hypotheses, not CEFR requirements.

## Source lanes

### Lane A — clear-rights anchors

Use owned, public-domain, or clearly licensed sources whose rights evidence can be reviewed independently of YouTube. These sources are useful for stable anchor clips, transcript provenance, and early technical validation.

A public-domain notice on a source page is still only a claim until a reviewer checks the item, contributor, third-party material, attribution requirements, and commercial/derivative-use implications.

### Lane B — creator-authorized YouTube conversations

Prioritize creator partnerships for interactional functions that are rare in public-domain broadcast interviews:

- asking another person's name;
- asking follow-up questions;
- requesting repetition;
- repairing misunderstanding;
- maintaining multiple turns.

Permission must explicitly cover the uses AtoEnglish needs. Permission to embed alone does not imply permission to store a transcript, create a derivative lesson, or use the material commercially.

### Lane C — owned unscripted elicitation

Use AtoEnglish-owned, unscripted conversation only to fill a measured corpus gap. Participants receive a real communicative prompt, not a line-by-line synthetic script. Lane C is a fallback, not a replacement for authentic external input.

## Discovery workflow

```text
capability gap
→ search source lanes
→ record candidate and evidence
→ reject obvious failures
→ rights review
→ watch/listen review
→ transcript and speaker review
→ propose clip windows
→ pedagogical review
→ treatment design
→ publication validator
→ learner pilot
```

## Hard rejection before media review

Reject a candidate when any of the following is already known:

- the source URL is unavailable or non-HTTPS;
- the item is not video or synchronized audiovisual media;
- no plausible target capability can be identified;
- the context is unsafe or inappropriate for the intended learner;
- the item clearly forbids the required use;
- the audio is known to be unusable;
- the proposed excerpt depends on misleading removal of context;
- the material cannot be attributed or traced to a source record.

## Candidate scoring

Every score is 0–5 and remains provisional until a human watches the media.

| Dimension | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `capabilityFit` | Directly demonstrates the target communicative action |
| `contextClarity` | Learner can infer who is speaking, where, and why |
| `audioClarity` | Speech is intelligible enough for the intended treatment |
| `naturalness` | Language is naturally produced rather than textbook recitation |
| `a0TreatmentPotential` | A short section can be scaffolded for an A0 learner |
| `speakerDiversityValue` | Adds a useful new voice, accent, pace, or context |
| `rightsEvidenceStrength` | Strength of the currently visible rights evidence |
| `transcriptEvidenceStrength` | Strength of transcript/caption evidence currently visible |

A high total score cannot override a failed rights gate.

## Review states

```text
discovered
→ needs_rights_review
→ needs_media_review
→ needs_transcript_review
→ shortlisted
→ accepted_for_authoring
→ rejected
```

`shortlisted` means worth spending human review time on. It does not mean learner-facing, legally cleared, or pedagogically approved.

## YouTube handling

For every candidate, record YouTube status separately:

- `confirmed`: exact watch URL verified;
- `needs_review`: a likely mirror or official upload has not been verified;
- `not_found`: no exact YouTube upload found yet;
- `not_required`: source is intentionally delivered through another permitted player.

The source-of-truth rights record may be a creator agreement, DVIDS page, government archive, or license page even when playback uses YouTube. A YouTube URL is not the rights evidence by itself.

## Candidate batch 1 finding

The first discovery batch intentionally begins with clear-rights government and NATO-associated interview material. It provides many usable self-introduction candidates, including multiple accents and contexts.

It also exposes a structural gap:

- `say one's name` is abundant;
- `greet someone` and `say where one is from` have some coverage;
- `ask another person's name` is scarce;
- `request repetition` is not represented by a strong candidate in the first batch.

This means AtoEnglish must not build the full A0 path solely from public-domain interview introductions. The next search round must prioritize creator-authorized natural interaction, especially repair and question-answer exchanges.

## Human review checklist

Before a candidate becomes `accepted_for_authoring`, reviewers must confirm:

1. exact source and playback URLs;
2. creator/publisher identity;
3. license or written permission;
4. allowed embedding, transcript storage, derivative lesson, commercial use, and self-hosting flags;
5. exact clip start and end;
6. transcript against audio;
7. speaker boundaries and proper names;
8. Vietnamese translation;
9. context and formality;
10. primary capability and prerequisites;
11. speech features;
12. safe and non-misleading extraction;
13. plausible comprehension, acquisition, and transfer treatment;
14. whether the clip adds useful variability rather than duplication.

## Separation of evidence

A candidate record may establish discovery evidence only. It must never be presented as proof of:

- final rights clearance;
- transcript accuracy;
- CEFR level;
- pedagogical effectiveness;
- learner progress.

Those require separate reviews and learner evidence.
