# AtoEnglish Product Truth — HISTORICAL

**Status:** historical product hypothesis  
**Superseded as current authority:** 2026-09-06 project reset  
**Canonical current state:** `docs/project/PROJECT_STATE.md`

This document previously defined a Vietnamese-first 28-day work-speaking pilot for adults who knew some English but froze when speaking at work.

That direction contained useful product discipline — especially one measurable learner outcome, bounded daily sessions, and separation of repository/usability/learning/market evidence — but it was never established as permanent product truth.

During the 2026-09-06 reset it is **not an active roadmap or current product definition**.

## Historical hypothesis

The former promise was roughly:

> Practice 10–15 minutes per day for 28 days to introduce yourself and your work, handle five predictable questions, and ask for repetition or slower speech.

The associated learning loop emphasized:

```text
real situation
→ short comprehensible model
→ useful chunks
→ controlled retrieval
→ supported speaking
→ reduced prompts
→ changed situation
→ concise feedback
→ immediate retry
→ later retrieval
```

## What may be reused

Future AtoEnglish work may reuse, with a new explicit decision:

- task-level can-do outcomes;
- short coherent sessions;
- required spoken output;
- prompt fading;
- feedback followed by retry;
- learner evidence separated from CI/technical evidence.

## What is not automatically current

- the 28-day duration;
- the workplace segment;
- the exact final speaking task;
- the five-question scope;
- old pilot success thresholds;
- the old ordered roadmap.

Historical details remain in Git history and are indexed in `docs/project/ARCHIVE_INDEX.md`.
